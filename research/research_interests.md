# Research Interests

My research interests focus on the design, evaluation, and efficient deployment of intelligent systems.

## Main Areas

- Multimodal Artificial Intelligence
- Computer Vision
- Vision-Language Models
- Large Language Models
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation
- Efficient AI Inference
- Trustworthy Machine Learning
- Robotics and Intelligent Systems

## Collaboration Preference

I am open to working on a new research project proposed by a host laboratory.

The project may be formally recognised as part of my Master's thesis, with an internal academic supervisor appointed by An-Najah National University.

I am particularly interested in funded research opportunities involving implementation, experimentation, model evaluation, data preparation, publication, and real-world AI applications.
